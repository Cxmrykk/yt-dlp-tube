Polling & Retention

How often every subscribed channel is checked for new uploads. The cache sweeper runs on the same schedule.

If a single poll turns up more unseen videos than this on a channel already being tracked, the far likelier cause is that YouTube changed its video IDs than that the channel uploaded that many at once. The channel is quietly re-catalogued instead of flooding the feed.

Feed Maintenance

The home feed is built from a per-channel baseline: the first time a channel is scanned, its existing uploads are catalogued and not shown. Only videos that appear afterwards reach the feed. Resetting the baseline forgets that catalogue for every channel — your subscriptions are left untouched, the feed empties, and it stays empty until genuinely new uploads arrive.
